Creator / Partner Terms & Conditions
AIVESTIRE
Please read the following terms carefully before onboarding as a partner on our platform.

1. Collaboration Model
The platform operates on a made-to-order model.
All products will be sold under the platform's brand name.
Designers and brands will act as production partners.
Only high-end couturier fashion designers are accepted.

2. Product Listing & Design
Only approved designs will be listed on the platform.
All designs must be original and must not be copied.
The platform reserves the right to accept, reject, or suggest modifications to any design.

3. Product Images & Submission
Clear, high-quality images are mandatory.
Required views: Front view, Back view, Side view.
Additional requirements: Close-up of fabric, Close-up of embroidery / print / detailing.
Images must show true colour — no heavy filters.
Poor-quality images may lead to rejection.

4. Orders & Production
Production begins only after order confirmation.
Simple designs: 5–7 days | Complex designs: 7–9 days.
Any delays must be communicated to the platform in advance.

5. Quality Check (QC) & Dispatch
All products will undergo a quality check (QC) by the platform team.
Products will be dispatched only after QC approval.
Products failing QC may be returned to the partner for correction.

6. Pricing & Commission
The platform will charge a commission on each order.
Commission structure: ₹0–₹1,000 → ₹150 | ₹1,001–₹2,000 → ₹250 | ₹2,001–₹5,000 → ₹400 | ₹5,001 and above → Custom (agreed upon onboarding).
You decide your product price — that amount is always yours in full.
The platform lists your product by adding its commission on top of your price.
The customer pays your price plus the platform commission.
You will always receive exactly the amount you set, with zero deductions from your decided price.
If your store or studio is outside Jaipur, shipping costs to the platform's warehouse will be borne by the partner.
In the event of a return due to a manufacturing defect or quality issue, reverse shipping costs will be borne by the partner.
Shipping costs for orders within Jaipur will be managed by the platform.

7. Payment Terms
Payments are processed only after successful delivery and completion of any return/replacement window.
In the event of returns, refunds, or disputes, the payout may be adjusted accordingly before release.
Payments are released on a fixed cycle — 17th of every month.
Payments will be transferred via bank transfer or UPI.

8. Fabric & Cost Responsibility
The partner is responsible for sourcing fabric and managing all production costs.
All costs related to fabric, stitching, and finishing are to be borne by the partner.

9. Size & Measurements
Accurate size charts must be provided by the partner.
Garments must match the selected size specifications.
Errors in sizing may lead to returns, and associated costs will be the partner's responsibility.

10. Quality & Finishing
The partner is solely responsible for fabric quality, stitching and finishing, and fit and construction.
All products must meet the platform's quality standards.

11. Returns & Replacements
In the event of manufacturing defects, the partner must repair or replace the product at their own cost.
After 1–2 return warnings due to quality issues or sizing errors, additional charges may be applied, including logistics and reverse shipping costs.
Repeated returns may result in a reduction of the partner's commission rate or a discount adjustment.

12. Liability & Refund Policy
The partner will be solely responsible for any manufacturing defects, quality issues, or sizing errors.
In such cases, the partner must bear the cost of repair, replacement, or refund, as applicable.
The platform will not be held liable for any losses arising due to partner-side errors, delays, or non-compliance with quality standards.

13. Shipping & Packaging
The platform manages delivery to the customer.
The partner must prepare the product within the agreed production timeline.
Proper packaging is required to prevent damage during transit.

14. Communication
Partners must respond within 24 hours to any queries or updates.
Timely communication is essential for smooth operations.

15. Sustainability (Encouraged)
The platform encourages the use of sustainable and skin-friendly fabrics.
Designers using such fabrics may receive additional visibility and benefits.

16. Performance Monitoring
Performance will be reviewed based on product quality, timely delivery, and customer returns.
Consistent performance is required to continue collaboration with the platform.

17. Rewards & Recognition
Designers with strong performance records (good quality, no QC failures, minimal returns) may receive higher visibility on the platform.
Top performers receive priority consideration for future collections and additional benefits and incentives.

18. Performance & Discontinuation Policy
Repeated QC failures or returns due to poor quality or colour mismatch (more than 1–2 occurrences in a month) may lead to discontinuation.
Repeated delays in production timelines (more than 2–3 occurrences in a month) may lead to discontinuation.
These measures are in place to maintain platform quality and a positive customer experience.

19. Order Delay & Cancellation
The partner must complete the order within the agreed production timeline.
In the event of a delay, the partner must inform the platform in advance.
If an order is delayed beyond the acceptable timeline and is cancelled by the customer, the partner may be held responsible for the resulting loss.
The platform reserves the right to deduct applicable costs, reduce future order allocation, or take action as per the performance policy.
Repeated delays leading to cancellations may result in discontinuation of the partnership.

20. Design Usage
Designs shared with the platform will be used solely for selling on the platform.
The platform will not share designs with other partners without prior consent.

21. Design Upload Guidelines
Each partner may upload a maximum of 30 unique designs as per the onboarding agreement.
A unique design refers to a distinct pattern, silhouette, or style.
Variations of the same design (such as different colourways) will be counted as one unique design.
The platform reserves the right to limit or curate design uploads to maintain quality and uniqueness.

22. Active Participation
Partners are expected to remain active and responsive at all times.
Inactive partners may be removed from the platform.

23. Termination
The platform reserves the right to discontinue collaboration if terms are violated or quality standards are not met.

24. Launch Selection
Onboarding is limited and selective.
Selection is based on design originality, quality, and production capability.

By proceeding with onboarding, you agree to all of the above terms and conditions.

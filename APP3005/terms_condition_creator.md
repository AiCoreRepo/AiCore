Creator / Partner Terms & Conditions
AIVESTIRE
Please read the following terms carefully before onboarding as a partner on our platform.

1. Collaboration Model
   The platform operates on a made-to-order model.
   All products will be sold under the platform’s brand name.
   Designers/brands will act as production partners.
   Allow only high end-couturier fashion designers are accepted.

2. Product Listing & Design
   Only approved designs will be listed on the platform.
   All designs must be original and not copied.
   The platform reserves the right to accept, reject, or suggest modifications to any design.

3. Product Images & Submission
   Clear and high-quality images are mandatory.
   Required views:
   Front view
   Back view
   Side view
   Additional requirements:
   Close-up of fabric
   Close-up of embroidery / print / detailing
   Images must show true color (no heavy filters).
   Poor-quality images may lead to rejection.

4. Orders & Production
   Production begins only after order confirmation.
   Expected timelines:
   Simple designs: 5–7 days
   Complex designs: 7–9 days
   Any delays must be communicated in advance.

5. Quality Check (QC) & Dispatch
   All products will undergo a quality check (QC) by the platform team.
   Products will be dispatched only after QC approval.
   Products failing QC may be returned for correction.

6. Pricing & Commission
   The platform will charge a commission on each order.
   Final payout = Product price – platform commission.

7. Payment Terms
   Payments will be released on a fixed cycle (e.g., 17th of every month).
   Payment is processed after order completion/delivery.
   Payments will be transferred via bank/UPI.

8. Fabric & Cost Responsibility
   The partner is responsible for sourcing fabric and managing production costs.
   All costs related to fabric, stitching, and finishing are borne by the partner.

9. Size & Measurements
   Accurate size charts must be provided.
   Garments must match the selected size.
   Errors in sizing may lead to returns and will be the partner’s responsibility.

10. Quality & Finishing
    The partner is responsible for:
    Fabric quality
    Stitching and finishing
    Fit and construction
    Products must meet platform quality standards.

11. Returns & Replacements
    In case of manufacturing defects, the partner must repair or replace the product.
    Return cases will be handled based on the issue.

12. Shipping & Packaging
    The platform manages delivery to the customer.
    The partner must prepare the product within the agreed timeline.
    Proper packaging is required to prevent damage.

13. Communication
    Partners must respond within 24 hours for any queries or updates.
    Timely communication is required for smooth operations.

14. Sustainability (Encouraged)
    The platform encourages the use of sustainable and skin-friendly fabrics.
    Designers using such fabrics may receive additional visibility and benefits.

15. Performance Monitoring
    Performance will be reviewed based on:
    Product quality
    Timely delivery
    Customer returns
    Consistent performance is required to continue collaboration.

16. Rewards & Recognition
    Designers with strong performance (good quality, no QC failures, minimal returns) may receive:
    Higher visibility
    Priority for future collections
    Additional benefits

17. Performance & Discontinuation Policy
    Repeated QC failures or returns due to poor quality or color mismatch (more than 1–2 times in a month) may lead to discontinuation.
    Repeated delays in production timelines (more than 2–3 times in a month) may lead to discontinuation.
    These measures are to maintain platform quality and customer experience. 19. Order Delay & Cancellation
    The partner must complete the order within the agreed production timeline.
    In case of delay, the partner must inform the platform in advance.
    If an order is delayed beyond the acceptable timeline and gets cancelled by the customer, the partner may be held responsible for the loss.
    In such cases, the platform reserves the right to:
    Deduct applicable costs (if any)
    Reduce future order allocation
    Take action as per the performance policy
    Repeated delays leading to cancellations may result in discontinuation of the partnership.

18. Design Usage
    Designs shared will be used only for selling on the platform.
    The platform will not share designs with other partners without consent. 21. Design Upload Guidelines
    Each partner can upload a limited number of 30 unique designs as per the onboarding agreement.
    A “unique design” refers to a distinct pattern, silhouette, or style.
    Variations of the same design (such as different colors of the same pattern) will be counted as one unique design.
    The platform reserves the right to limit or curate design uploads to maintain quality and uniqueness on the platform.

19. Active Participation
    Partners are expected to remain active and responsive.
    Inactive partners may be removed from the platform.

20. Termination
    The platform reserves the right to discontinue collaboration if terms are violated or standards are not met.

21. Launch Selection
    Onboarding is limited.
    Selection is based on design originality, quality, and production capability.

By proceeding with onboarding, you agree to all the above terms and conditions
